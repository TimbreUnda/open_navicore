# Code of conduct

Open NaviCore contributors are expected to keep technical collaboration
welcoming, precise, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and assume good faith.
- Critique claims and evidence, not people.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, and authorization boundaries.
- Help newcomers understand the ABI and safety constraints of the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate publication of
credentials or personal data are not acceptable. Project maintainers may edit,
hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate these
standards.

Report conduct concerns privately to an organization owner through GitHub.
